How booking works

Three steps. No phone tag.

All enquiries and bookings come through the form on this page, so nothing gets lost on a voicemail and we have your list in writing before the visit.

1

Send your list

Name, number, what needs doing and the day that suits. A photo or two by reply helps us bring the right fixings.

2

Time and price

We come back with a slot and a price from the published rate card, or a free fixed quote for bigger jobs. A minimum £50 non-refundable deposit secures the booking.

3

The visit

Seven days a week: weekdays 8am–5pm, Saturdays 9–5 and Sundays 11–4, weekends subject to availability. We work through the list and tidy as we go. Pay the balance by cash, debit or credit card on the day.

Privacy policy

Your details, plainly.

Who we are

Watford Handyman, Watford WD17 2PZ, is the data controller for this website. Contact: enquiries@watfordhandyman.uk.

What we collect

Only what you type into the booking form: your name, mobile and telephone numbers, email address, the address where the work is, your jobs list and your preferred day.

Why, and on what basis

To reply to your enquiry, agree a time and a price and carry out the work. The lawful basis is taking steps at your request before entering a contract, then performing it.

Who sees it

The family who run the business. Form submissions are delivered to our inbox by a form-processing service acting on our instructions. We never sell or share your details for marketing.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become a booking are deleted within 6 months. Records of completed work are kept for 6 years for tax and warranty purposes.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing enquiries@watfordhandyman.uk. You also have the right to complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk).

Cookies

This site sets no tracking or advertising cookies. Fonts and some photographs are loaded from Google Fonts and Unsplash, and the live review score is loaded from Trustpilot; each may see your IP address when it serves those files.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a fresh date.
